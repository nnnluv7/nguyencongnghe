# NCN Blast · 29 October 2026

Adds `/ghep-khoi/` as the eighth NCN Arcade game. The existing Liquid Glass shell, login, announcements, Admin tools and other game engines remain intact. The homepage/menu, Arcade hub, Library seed, search catalogue and sitemap include the new route. The last hub row has two balanced cards on desktop and remains two columns on narrow screens.

The choice follows the block-placement genre: Poki's homepage listed Blocky Blast Puzzle under “Popular this week” when checked on 29 October 2026 (https://poki.com/ and https://poki.com/en/g/blocky-blast-puzzle). NCN Blast has its own code, SVG art, sounds, branding and scoring; no third-party game, assets or runtime are embedded.

## Play

Place one of three fixed shapes onto an 8×8 board. Complete rows and columns clear simultaneously; intersections count once, and remaining tiles do not fall. A new hand arrives after all three pieces are used. A run ends when no remaining piece fits, rather than when just one of them is blocked.

- Classic: no timer, deterministic seeded deals with a witnessed legal placement sequence for each new hand. A different player choice can still block a hand.
- Today: the same Vietnam-date-seeded piece sequence for everyone, independent of earlier moves, with a limit of 60 placements. Classic and daily ranking boards are separate, and each daily date has its own board.
- Each placed cell earns 5 points. Clears earn a line bonus, multiple-line bonus and combo multiplier; an empty board adds 300. A combo survives two non-clearing placements and resets on the third.
- Three hints per run preview a legal move without placing it or awarding points. The player must confirm the move.

Mouse and touch support drag-and-drop with a board-sized ghost and legal footprint. Touch lifts the ghost above the finger. Alternatively, select a piece then tap the top-left cell. Keyboard: 1/2/3 selects, arrows position, Enter or Space places, H hints, P pauses, F toggles fullscreen, Escape cancels selection or drag. Board scrolling is not intercepted; only the draggable piece cards capture their gesture.

The board, partial hand, seeded generator, score and remaining hints save after each move and restore paused. Replacing a saved run requires an in-game confirmation. A finished run removes the active save. Storage restrictions do not prevent quick play. Procedural sound defaults off; reduced motion and dark mode are supported. No gameplay render loop or additional runtime library is required.

## Shared services

Uses the existing NCN Arcade identity, profile and scores handler as game `blast`. Guest and member behavior, origin checks, owner-bound server-issued runs, rate limits and preview restrictions are unchanged. Quick play makes no profile/run/score POST. There are no stages, so ranking submissions use level 0. Score plausibility remains bounded by the server's per-game ceiling; this is not server-authoritative game replay.

All overlays, dialogs and ranking controls stay inside the fullscreen root. Fullscreen uses the existing native/fallback helper; the immersive grid resets to one centered column. The board retains intrinsic square dimensions at welcome, play, pause and saved welcome, including phone landscape.

## Validation

`tests/ncn-blast.test.mjs` covers the engine's shape bounds, deterministic daily sequence, simultaneous clearing, score accounting, hand consumption/refill, legal hints, loss and 60-move completion, persistence validation and randomized runs. The actual scores handler has a new integration case for both modes and date isolation.

`tests/browser/ncn-blast.mjs` exercises real mouse/touch/keyboard input, earned row clearing, exact mid-hand resume, shared-profile saving, blocked storage, dark/reduced-motion mode, scroll behavior, menu/help pause and native/fallback fullscreen across desktop and phone orientations. Network fixtures block all external writes. The shared Arcade journey also plays the eighth game under one profile.
